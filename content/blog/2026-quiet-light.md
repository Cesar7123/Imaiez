## Let the moment arrive

The best portraits rarely begin with a pose. They begin with room: room to breathe, notice, and let a real expression surface.

For a recent session, we left the shot list behind and walked until the afternoon light softened. The camera became part of the conversation, not its center.

> The image changes when the person in front of you feels seen.

### Three small choices

- Start with a walk instead of a location brief.
- Look for reflected light before adding anything artificial.
- Leave space between frames so the story can unfold.

The result is quieter, but never empty. Texture, gesture, and the little pauses carry the memory.
