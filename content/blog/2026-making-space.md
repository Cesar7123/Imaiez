## The frame after the frame

Every session needs structure. It also needs a little unclaimed time. That is where the accidental gestures, windblown hair, and honest laughter tend to appear.

I now build a short pause into every shoot. We change nothing. We simply stop directing and pay attention.

### A practical reset

1. Put the camera down for one minute.
2. Ask what felt most like the person, not what looked most polished.
3. Return to the light with one simple prompt.

Photography can be precise without becoming rigid. A clear intention gives surprise somewhere to land.
